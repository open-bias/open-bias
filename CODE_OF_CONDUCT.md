# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want OpenBias to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive project for everyone.
As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for people of every background, identity, and level of
experience.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive community include:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Being clear, patient, and kind when discussing issues or reviewing work.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or actions.
- Personal attacks, trolling, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Repeated disruption of discussions, reviews, issues, or pull requests.
- Any conduct that maintainers reasonably determine to be inappropriate in a
  professional open source community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, documentation, community channels, and project-related
events. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests,
and other contributions that are not aligned with this code of conduct.

Maintainers may take temporary or permanent action when needed, including warnings,
temporary restrictions, removal from community spaces, or a ban from participation.
Enforcement decisions should be made fairly, consistently, and with respect for the
privacy and safety of everyone involved.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project
maintainers. Use a public GitHub issue only if it is safe and appropriate to do so.
For sensitive reports, contact the maintainers privately through the project's
published contact channels.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated as promptly and fairly as possible.
Maintainers are expected to respect the privacy and security of the reporter and
any person affected.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted from common open source community standards,
including the Contributor Covenant.
